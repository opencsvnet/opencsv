Identity and issuance

The exact instrument is identified by its OpenCSV asset_id, not by the letters “USD.” Its genesis commits these terms and the issuer public key. Issuance requires a proof of control of that genesis-bound issuer key and a Bitcoin-ordered OpenCSV anchor.

Wallet policy

  • The preview Signal wallet exposes no arbitrary instrument creator.
  • Its first “Issue test USD” action creates this fixed definition, secures the updated checkpoint in Signal Backup, and only then prepares issuance.
  • Legacy ticker-only or custom prototype assets remain recoverable and readable but cannot be issued or sent by the USD Preview product flow.
  • Bitcoin in Signal is restricted to OpenCSV protocol fees; it is not a general-purpose BTC send wallet.

Future production issuer

A future Tether-issued OpenCSV instrument would be a separately authenticated issuer, terms document, asset identity, and protocol version. Preview balances will never be silently relabeled or converted. Any future migration or redemption must be explicit, issuer-authorized, and auditable.

No production claim

This page documents developer test behavior. It is not an offer, payment promise, custodial account, representation of reserves, or guarantee of availability. Mainnet creation and broadcast remain disabled pending separate issuer authorization, security review, and owner approval.
